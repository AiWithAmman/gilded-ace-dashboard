# Luck, Location, or a Leak? — The Gilded Ace bottom-10 review

**Verdict: don't replace the bottom 10.** None of them is weaker than identical games at the same spot. Nine sit in Back Forty, three were on the floor only part of the period, and one, M-1158, is a likely payout leak to inspect.

**Method.** Each machine's gap to the median splits exactly into game, time on floor, downtime, location, machine and luck. Location uses a zone × month × weekday benchmark. The machine index (theo ÷ benchmark) has a 90% interval. Luck z = (house win − theo) ÷ √(Σbet² × payout variance).

- **Location.** Back Forty earns about 34% less theo than the same game elsewhere. It's causal: M-1152 moved to the Main Entrance and its theo per hour rose from $14.3 to $29.4, and during the walkway closure Back Forty fell 25% while the rest of the floor rose 6%.
- **Luck.** 24 of 310 machines sit beyond |z| = 1.96 (about 16 by chance, the excess in jackpot-heavy games); only M-1158 passes a multiple-testing cut-off.
- **Leak.** M-1158 hits on 38.6% of spins against a configured 31.5% (z ≈ 124), $36K below theo. Inspect it; this is not proof of wrongdoing.
- **Truly weak.** The only machine clearly below its peers, M-1184 (index 0.80), isn't on the list.

**Floor.** Demand eased (June −6% per day vs February), tracking real LVCVA visitation. Downtime cost about $94K of theo (0.4%); faults wait about 2 h on the one-tech grave shift against 0.6 h when two techs are on. Tracked players wagered $163.8M and lost $15.6M, in line with theo. Comps cost $191K. On May 1 the points charged per comp dollar fell from 13.1 to 5.8, with no lift in play.

**Model.** Trained Jan–Apr, tested May–Jun, a weekday × event forecast cut daily coin-in MAE from $218K (constant baseline) to $64K; a trend model overfit and was rejected. It sets each action's July baseline and points technician hours at weekends and grave shifts.

**Plan: spend $12,700, keep $87,300.**
1. Inspect M-1158 ($1,200): ~$4K theo in 30 days (range $2.3K–$5.5K). Success = hit rate within 0.5 pt of 31.5%.
2. Back Forty signage ($7,500): ~$13K (range $2.6K–$26K), measured by difference-in-differences against the other zones.
3. Comp pilot with a control group ($4,000; no added comp cost) to decide whether to reverse May 1.

Leasing 10 replacements would cost $33,800 for about $1.6K of July theo.
